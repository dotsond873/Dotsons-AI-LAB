Install on Android

  1. Tap Download Shady for Android.
  2. Open Shady.apk from your downloads.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app, then tap Install.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Your browser chats stay in that browser. The installed app saves its own chats.

Using an iPhone?

Open Shady in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
